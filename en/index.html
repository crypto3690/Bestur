Before you book

Clear information for planning your private service

A direct summary of what BESTUR does and how the process works before a service is confirmed.

Who is BESTUR?

BESTUR is a San Miguel de Allende tour operator coordinating private transportation, airport transfers, cultural tours, winery transportation, weddings, groups and special routes.

Where do we operate?

Our main operation starts in San Miguel de Allende and connects with Guanajuato, Querétaro, Mexico City, key airports and other destinations in central Mexico.

Who is the service for?

International travelers, couples, families, groups, wedding guests and clients looking for clear, comfortable and dependable private transportation.

How is a service confirmed?

After reviewing the request, BESTUR sends a confirmation with a folio and final operating details. The service is fully confirmed when the client receives that confirmation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before requesting an airport transfer, private tour or special service.

How do I request a quote?

Complete the form and send the request by WhatsApp or email with your date, service, number of passengers and the main details of your route or experience.

Do you provide airport transfers?

Yes. BESTUR coordinates private transfers to and from BJX, QRO and Mexico City Airport. AIFA is also available upon request.

Are the tours private?

Yes. Tours are coordinated privately for couples, families and groups looking for a more personalized experience.

What transportation does BESTUR coordinate for weddings and groups?

BESTUR coordinates arrivals, departures and local movements for couples, families, guests and groups between airports, hotels or Airbnbs, churches, venues and other event points. BESTUR does not manage accommodations or hotel reservations.

Where is BESTUR located?

BESTUR is located at Carlos del Castillo 6, Guadalupe, 37710 San Miguel de Allende, Guanajuato, Mexico.

When is my booking fully confirmed?

A request or quote does not confirm the service. BESTUR will send a confirmation with the folio and final details; once you receive it, the service is fully confirmed.
