Frequently asked questions

Before traveling to the airport

Does BESTUR monitor my flight?

Yes. For arrivals, we use the official time published by the airline as the reference and monitor changes to adjust pickup if the flight arrives early or is delayed.

How early should I leave San Miguel de Allende?

As an operational reference, we recommend leaving 3 hours and 30 minutes before the flight time for BJX and QRO, and 7 hours before the flight time for MEX/AICM and AIFA.

Which vehicle will be used?

For airport transfers, we consider both passengers and luggage: Sedan for 1–2 passengers, Suburban for 3–6, Hiace/Transit for 7–10 and Crafter for 11–16. Larger groups require a special quote.

Does the price change if I pay by card or need an invoice?

The cash or bank transfer reference corresponds to the direct rate. For card payment or invoice, 16% VAT is added.

When is my transfer confirmed?

A request or quote does not automatically confirm the service. The transfer is fully confirmed when BESTUR sends the confirmation with a reference number and final operational details.
