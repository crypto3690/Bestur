Frequently asked questions

Before traveling between QRO and San Miguel

How long is the transfer between QRO and San Miguel de Allende?

The approximate travel time is 1 hour and 15 minutes. Actual travel time may vary depending on traffic and road conditions.

Does BESTUR monitor my flight if it is delayed or arrives early?

Yes. For arrivals, we use the airline’s official arrival time as the reference and monitor flight updates so the airport pickup can be adjusted.

How early should I leave San Miguel de Allende for QRO Airport?

BESTUR uses 3 hours and 30 minutes before the flight as the base. The suggested time is rounded down to the previous 5-minute interval, adding up to 4 additional minutes of operating buffer.

Is the transfer private?

Yes. Your request is for private BESTUR transportation.

Which vehicle is recommended for my group?

For airport transfers, we consider both passengers and luggage: Sedan up to 2 passengers, Suburban up to 6, Hiace/Transit up to 10 and Crafter up to 16. The final vehicle is confirmed based on availability.

Can I also book the return transfer to the airport?

Yes. You can request the arrival transfer, return transfer or both. BESTUR confirms each service according to date, schedule and availability.
